# SUBFLOW: Judge Presentation & Hackathon Demo Script
**Platform:** Brightloop Software Subscription Lifecycle & Recurring Billing  
**Duration:** 5 - 7 minutes  

---

## 🎬 Act 1: Executive Dashboard & MRR Architecture (1 min)
1. **Navigate to Dashboard:**
   - Point out the 6 KPI Stat Cards: Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), Active Subscriptions, Total Customers, Collected Revenue, Outstanding Receivables, Overdue Invoices.
   - Explain MRR normalization formula:
     - Daily micro-tier: $\times 30$
     - Weekly sprint: $\times 4.333$
     - Monthly standard: $\times 1$
     - Annual platinum: $\div 12$
   - Point out the Revenue Collections area chart (Recharts) and Subscription Status donut chart.
   - Show Upcoming Renewals and Recent Invoices table.

---

## 🎬 Act 2: Product Catalogue & Variant Pricing (1 min)
1. **Navigate to "Products & Variants":**
   - Click **"Variants"** button on `Brightloop Core Platform`.
   - Highlight:
     - Base Product Price: **₹2,000.00**
     - Odoo Edition Variant: Surcharge **+₹560.00**
     - Calculated Final Price: **₹2,560.00**
   - Click **"Add Variant"** to demonstrate creating a new variant on the fly (e.g. "Enterprise High-Availability Tier" with +₹1,200).
   - Point out that only Admins can create/edit catalogue data (switch to Staff role to demonstrate read-only banner).

---

## 🎬 Act 3: Plan Management & Frequencies (45 sec)
1. **Navigate to "Plans":**
   - Show the 4 billing frequencies: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, and **Yearly**.
   - Click **"Create Plan"** or view existing plans to highlight the fine-grained lifecycle permission flags:
     - Pause allowed?
     - Early close / cancel allowed?
     - Renewal allowed?
     - Auto-close upon expiry?

---

## 🎬 Act 4: Quotation Conversion & Expiry Protection (1 min)
1. **Navigate to "Quotations":**
   - Point out `QUO-2026-0041` (Accepted) and `QUO-2026-0038` (Expired).
   - Show that for the expired quote, the **"Convert to Sub"** button is disabled, proving requirement: *"Prevent conversion of expired quotations"*.
   - Click **"Convert to Sub"** on `QUO-2026-0041`.
   - Watch the instant toast notification: quotation is converted into a confirmed/active subscription with generated number `SUB-2026-XXXXX` and product lines transferred!

---

## 🎬 Act 5: Subscription Lifecycle Engine & Recurring Billing (1.5 min)
1. **Navigate to "Subscriptions":**
   - See all subscriptions across lifecycle states: Draft, Confirmed, Active, Paused, Closed.
   - Click **"Pause"** on an active subscription → modal confirms pause according to plan rules.
   - Click **"Resume"** on a paused subscription → contract is re-activated.
   - Now click the green **"Run Billing Now"** button in the header or top bar:
     - The transactional recurring billing engine executes immediately.
     - Detects all active subscriptions due for billing (`next_billing_date <= today`).
     - Uses idempotency key `subscription_id + billing_period` to prevent duplicate billing!
     - Advances the `next_billing_date` according to plan frequency.
     - Generates new invoices in the invoice ledger.
   - Click **"Run Billing Now"** again: observe that 0 duplicates are generated!

---

## 🎬 Act 6: Invoice Management & Atomic Payment Settlement (1 min)
1. **Navigate to "Invoices":**
   - Observe the new recurring invoices generated by the billing engine.
   - Click **"Print"** on any invoice to view the tax invoice sheet formatted with GST breakdown and corporate headers.
   - Click **"Record Payment"** on an unsettled invoice:
     - Notice the modal displays Invoice Total, Already Settled, and Remaining Balance.
     - Enter a partial payment amount (e.g. ₹1,000 against a ₹2,560 invoice).
     - Submit: notice the invoice updates to `Confirmed` and balance reflects remaining ₹1,560.
     - Record second payment for remaining ₹1,560: invoice automatically transitions to **Paid**!
     - Try entering an amount greater than the balance: system strictly rejects overpayment!

---

## 🎬 Act 7: Customer Self-Service Portal (1 min)
1. **Click "Customer" in the Header Role Switcher:**
   - The UI transforms into the **Customer Self-Service Portal** for Apex Dynamics Ltd.
   - Observe strict data isolation:
     - Only Apex Dynamics subscriptions and invoices are visible.
     - Customer can view active contracts, self-service request pause or early close, and settle invoices directly via **"Pay Now"**.
     - Customer has zero access to admin catalogue, other clients, or staff settings.
2. **Return to "Admin" role:**
   - Watch Dashboard update with the newly recorded payments, increased collected cash, and updated MRR.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
SUBFLOW delivers an end-to-end, resilient subscription lifecycle and recurring billing platform for Brightloop Software, exceeding every functional requirement with robust PostgreSQL architecture, RLS security, and an enterprise SaaS design.
